Refine the Mac app after a design council review. Problems that need attention carry the system caution symbol, failures open their Error Details, and an unreadable settings file appears as a caution row in Models with its location. The toolbar leaves a pane's name to its header until the header scrolls away, sidebar icons follow the system sidebar icon size, and rows read as single elements to VoiceOver. Scaffold panes say plainly what Pico doesn't do yet, Sesh's status rows read Not checked, and Homebrew confirmations warn that closing Pico stops the action.

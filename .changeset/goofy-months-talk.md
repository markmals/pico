Bring back OMP's `todo` tool, shown in Sesh as the session's plan: a flat task list in the Plan panel above the message box. Successful updates change only the plan; a failed or cancelled update appears as a failed tool call. Dropped items leave the plan, blocked items show as pending with their reason, and reopening a conversation shows the agent's current list. Pico always offers the tool and never sends OMP's todo reminders.

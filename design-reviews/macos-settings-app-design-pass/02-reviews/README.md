# 02 — Independent reviews

**Reads:** The frozen brief, evidence index, and each reviewer's own specialist skill.

**Produces:** One Markdown finding report per applicable lens. Each reviewer works independently and returns at most five findings and a bounded lens score with evidence limits.

**Check before handoff:** All applicable lenses have a report; exclusions have a reason. Findings cite specific evidence and sources, separate observation from inference, and do not claim unperformed tests. Do not share one reviewer's findings with another before first-pass reports are complete.

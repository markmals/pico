# 03 — Deliberation

**Reads:** All independent reviews and the frozen brief.

**Produces:** `shortlist.md` clustering duplicate findings and proposing a prioritized plan; `votes.md` identifying who proposed that order and recording each reviewer’s plan vote and material qualifications. Distinguish independent findings from endorsement of the circulated order; preserve item votes when actually given.

**Check before handoff:** At least 80% of participating reviewers support the plan and priorities. Preserve dissent and domain findings. If agreement is absent, show alternatives and the decision needed. Critical accessibility or core-task failures remain open regardless of agreement.
